## Breakout video patterns
- **Hooks:** a counterintuitive claim stated in the first second ("Imagine a star so dense that a teaspoon weighs a billion tons", "Stars twinkle, planets don't", "Watch this"). The text hook on screen matches the spoken hook.
- **Length:** loopable shorts of 14–25 s are breaking out on IG. TikTok explainers that work run 45–80 s. YouTube Shorts in this niche run up to 3 min.
- **Formats:** (1) faceless footage with a static or guiding text overlay plus a voiceover, which matches our pipeline; (2) side-by-side comparison infographics (star vs planet); (3) green-screen talking head over NASA imagery; (4) split-screen reaction to a viral claim, often myth-busting ("Earth isn't where it was in 1979").
- **Effective loops:** the end of the video circles back to the hook visual.

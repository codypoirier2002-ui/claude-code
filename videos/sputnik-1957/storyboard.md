# Sputnik's beep started the space age (1957) — storyboard

## Verified facts
- Sputnik 1 launched and entered Earth orbit on Oct. 4, 1957 (https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/sputnik-1/).
- Launched from the Soviet rocket testing site near Tyuratam, Kazakh Republic; carried a small radio beacon that beeped at regular intervals (https://www.nasa.gov/history/sputnik/sputorig.html).
- Pressurized aluminum-alloy sphere (image-article page above).
- Weighed 184 pounds, orbited about every 90 minutes, signal monitored by amateur radio operators worldwide; "beep-beep" transmitter, no scientific instruments (https://www.nasa.gov/history/65-years-ago-sputnik-ushers-in-the-space-age/).
- Launch "shocked the world" and began the Space Age (image-article page).
- Not used: 58 cm diameter / 96-minute orbit (sources disagree or not confirmed).

## Shots (artist's reenactment, ~30 s)
1. Hook 0–3 s — "One tiny beeping ball changed the world." Sputnik spins, beep rings pulse, BEEP labels pop. Slow push-in. Deep blue/gold.
2. Launch 3–8 s — "On October fourth, 1957 ... launched Sputnik One into orbit." Rocket on pad, liftoff burst, flash+shake, camera tilts up. Warm sunset.
3. Orbit 8–14 s — "184 pounds, circling Earth every ninety minutes." Sputnik orbits toy Earth along gold path; labels 184 LB / EVERY 90 MIN. Blue.
4. Listen 14–19 s — "Its only job was a radio beep that amateur radio operators everywhere could hear." Radio listeners cheer on the ground as Sputnik passes overhead beeping. Purple night.
5. Payoff 19–25 s — "The world was shocked. The Space Age had begun. Beep." Flash + burst, SPACE AGE label, final BEEP echoing the hook. Pink/violet.

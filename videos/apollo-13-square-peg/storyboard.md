# Apollo 13: the square peg in a round hole (1970)

Format: history reenactment (stylized 3D, "artist's reenactment"). Target 40-45 s.

## Verified facts (opened and read 2026-11-02)
Sources:
- NASA, "Apollo 13: Mission Details" https://www.nasa.gov/missions/apollo/apollo-13-mission-details/
- NASA, "Apollo 13: The Successful Failure" https://www.nasa.gov/missions/apollo/apollo-13-the-successful-failure/
- NASA, "50 Years Ago: Apollo 13 Review Board Report" https://www.nasa.gov/history/50-years-ago-apollo-13-review-board-report/

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Apollo 13 launched April 11, 1970 to attempt the third crewed Moon landing; crew Lovell, Haise, Swigert | Mission Details |
| An oxygen tank (No. 2) exploded on April 13, 1970, about 200,000 miles from Earth (the Review Board page says 205,000); ~55 h 55 min into the flight | Mission Details; Review Board |
| The crew used the lunar module Aquarius as a "lifeboat" | Successful Failure |
| The LM was designed to support two men for two days and was asked to care for three for about four days | Mission Details |
| After about a day and a half in the LM a warning light showed carbon dioxide had built up to a dangerous level | Mission Details |
| The command module's lithium hydroxide CO2 canisters were square; the LM's openings were round | Mission Details; Successful Failure |
| Mission control devised a way to attach the CM canisters to the LM system with plastic bags, cardboard and tape from on board; NASA: "a square peg in a round hole" | Mission Details; Successful Failure |
| Splashdown April 17, 1970 in the Pacific near Samoa; crew picked up by USS Iwo Jima; NASA calls the mission a "successful failure" | Mission Details; Successful Failure |

Not claimed: any dialogue, exact socks/manual/etc. details, number of people on the ground.

## Shots (6)
Narration is ~100 words, spoken in 6 beats.

1. **hook** (0-3 s) `shots/squareround.html`. VO: "A square filter. A round hole." Mint-and-coral palette on deep-space navy. A chunky square canister floats in and is pushed at a round porthole ring; it clunks and bounces, shake. Labels SQUARE / ROUND. Camera push-in. Badge "1970".
2. **bang** (3-11 s) `shots/bang.html`. VO: "April 13th, 1970. Apollo 13 is about two hundred thousand miles from Earth when an oxygen tank explodes." A toy-like Apollo stack (cone, cylinder, lunar module box) cruises, Earth tiny behind. Flash + shake + venting particles. Label "200,000 MILES". Mood: calm teal, then hot orange.
3. **lifeboat** (11-21 s) `shots/lifeboat.html`. VO: "The three astronauts crowd into the lunar module, Aquarius, built for two men for two days. Now it must keep three alive for about four." Gold LM; two figures then a third pops in; labels "2 MEN / 2 DAYS" turn into "3 MEN / ~4 DAYS". Mood: warm gold on dark.
4. **problem** (21-29 s) `shots/co2.html` + `shots/squareround.html`. VO: "After a day and a half, a warning light shows carbon dioxide at dangerous levels. The spare filters are square. The openings are round." Red pulsing warning light, "CO2 HIGH" label; crossfade to the square/round callback.
5. **fix** (29-36 s) `shots/adapter.html`. VO: "Engineers in Houston devise a fix from plastic bags, cardboard and tape. A square peg, in a round hole." Parts (bag, cardboard, tape) fly together into an adapter; canister slots into the ring; green flash.
6. **payoff** (36-43 s) `shots/splash.html`. VO: "On April 17th, all three astronauts splash down safely in the Pacific. NASA calls it a successful failure." Capsule under parachutes drops into a vivid ocean, splash burst, three figures cheer, label "ALL 3 HOME". Final frame pays off the hook with the green-lit round filter.

Music: Kevin MacLeod bed (CC BY 4.0), low under VO. Crossfades 0.25 s between clips; flash/shake on the explosion.

# Apollo 8 Earthrise (1968): storyboard
Verified facts (opened sources):
- Launched Dec. 21, 1968; first crewed Saturn V launch; first humans to leave low Earth orbit (nasa.gov/mission/apollo-8/).
- Dec. 24, 1968 Borman, Lovell, Anders were the first humans to orbit the Moon and first to see Earthrise (same page; nasa.gov/image-article/apollo-8-astronaut-bill-anders-captures-earthrise/).
- Earthrise came as Apollo 8 rounded the far side for the fourth time; it was unseen on three earlier orbits; Borman: "it's not scheduled"; they hunted for color film; one of Anders's three photos became iconic (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov .../earthrise_in_4k_transcript.html).
Shots (voice from ElevenLabs): 1 hook 3.4s Earth pops over Moon, flash, drifting craft, blue/violet. 2 launch 7s rocket lifts off, burst+shake, camera tilts up, sunset orange. 3 orbit 6s craft circles Moon, mint trail, label. 4 rise 7.4s Earth climbs behind lunar horizon, flash, "NOT ON THE SCHEDULE". 5 photo 9s big Earth over horizon, flashes, label EARTHRISE (payoff of hook).

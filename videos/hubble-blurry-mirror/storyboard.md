# Hubble launched with a blurry mirror (1990) — storyboard

## Verified facts (sources opened)
- Hubble launched in 1990 on Space Shuttle Discovery (STS-31), April 24, 1990. NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/overview/the-history-of-hubble/ ; STS-31 date: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STS-31 (cites NASA STS-31 archive)
- NASA announced the flaw on June 27, 1990, two months after launch; the primary mirror was too flat near its outer edge; error 10x larger than tolerance. https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/observatory/design/optics/hubbles-mirror-flaw/
- Cause: contractors improperly set up the null corrector (test device); lens spacing off by 1.3 mm. Same NASA page.
- Mirror flattened by 2.2 microns, roughly one-fiftieth the thickness of a human hair. https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/observatory/missions-to-hubble/servicing-mission-1/ ; Smithsonian NASM https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/repairing-hubble
- Mirror was not replaceable; COSTAR (small corrective mirrors) and WFPC2 installed on Servicing Mission 1, Dec 2-13, 1993. https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/overview/the-history-of-hubble/
- Every instrument installed since has built-in corrective optics. SM1 page above.

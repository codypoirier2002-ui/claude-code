# Wright brothers' first flight (1903)
Verified (NASA, Smithsonian NASM, Library of Congress):
- Dec. 17, 1903, Kill Devil Hills near Kitty Hawk, NC; Orville piloted the first flight at 10:35 a.m.: 12 s, 120 ft, top speed 6.8 mph.
- Four flights that day, brothers taking turns; the fourth, Wilbur at the controls: 59 s, 852 ft.
Sources: https://www.nasa.gov/history/120-years-ago-the-first-powered-flight-at-kitty-hawk/ · https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/1903-wright-flyer/nasm_A19610048000 · https://www.loc.gov/item/today-in-history/december-17/

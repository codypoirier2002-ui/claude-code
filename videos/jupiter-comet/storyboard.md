# A comet smashed into Jupiter (1994): storyboard

Artist's reenactment: stylized, not to scale. Narration: ElevenLabs (Liam). Music: "Floating Cities" by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0.

## Verified facts (opened and read)
Source A: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/comets/p-shoemaker-levy-9/
Source B: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/galileo/
- Discovered in 1993, already torn into more than 20 pieces orbiting Jupiter (A).
- Close approach to Jupiter in July 1992; torn apart by tidal forces from Jupiter's gravity (A).
- Fragments labeled A through W; NASA calls them a "freight train" (A).
- Impacts July 16 to 22, 1994 (A).
- For the first time in history NASA had spacecraft in position to watch a collision between two solar system bodies; Galileo, still en route to Jupiter, captured direct views (A).
- Fragment W's night-side impact imaged by Galileo on July 22, 1994 (A, B).
- Force of 300 million atomic bombs; plumes 1,200 to 1,900 miles (2,000 to 3,000 km) high (A).
- Dark ringed scars were eventually erased by Jupiter's winds; dust let scientists track high-altitude winds on Jupiter for the first time (A).

## Shots
1. hook (4.9 s) "A comet was torn into a line of pieces, all aimed at Jupiter." Train of glowing fragments streams in toward banded Jupiter; badge 1994. Camera slow push-in. Deep navy/violet.
2. split (6 s) "In July 1992 it flew so close that Jupiter's gravity ripped it apart." One nucleus swings past Jupiter, flash and shake, splits into a string; label TORN APART BY GRAVITY; badge 1992. Navy/coral.
3. freight (7.7 s) "Astronomers found it in 1993: more than twenty pieces, labeled A through W, like a freight train." 21 fragments in a row, labels A, W, 21 PIECES; camera pans along; badge 1993. Cold blue.
4. impact (11.5 s) "From July 16 to 22, 1994, the pieces hit. For the first time, NASA had spacecraft in place to watch..." Fragments hit one by one, bursts, atmosphere glow, scars; small Galileo probe labeled; badge 1994 July 16 to 22. Magenta/orange.
5. plumes (7.8 s) "The blasts threw plumes up to 3,000 kilometers high, with the force of 300 million atomic bombs." Mushroom plumes rise from the limb; labels UP TO 3,000 km, 300 MILLION ATOMIC BOMBS; flash and shake. Hot orange.
6. scars (7.4 s) "Jupiter's winds erased the scars..." Dark ringed scars appear, then fade as wind streaks sweep over; final frame: calm Jupiter (pays off the hook). Blue/amber.
